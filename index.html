About

I have been passionate about fashion since childhood. It's my favorite way of expressing myself, and it's where I can apply my knowledge and skills.

I'm a pattern maker based in Vilnius, with four years of making patterns for women's clothing, from first draft to sampling, fittings and production. I'm now coming back to fashion and building my skills in 3D garment design with CLO 3D. Outside work I knit, run, read and garden.
